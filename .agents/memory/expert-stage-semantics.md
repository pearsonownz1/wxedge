---
name: Expert stage semantics
description: Shared six-stage workflow and the distinction between approval, contact and closure.
---
All expert categories use exactly New, Contacted, Followed Up, Responded, Meeting Set, and Closed.

Legacy Approved to Send maps to New, not Contacted. Closed includes both won and lost outcomes; it is not proof that an expert responded or agreed to a meeting.

**Why:** The user requested a simpler shared workflow based on completed contact, follow-ups, replies, meetings and closure. Approval alone does not establish that a message was sent, and combining positive and negative outcomes must not inflate response or meeting metrics.

**How to apply:** Keep the same stages across categories, forms, filters and CSV imports. Preserve original stage history during consolidation and use that history, rather than Closed alone, when interpreting successful outcomes.
